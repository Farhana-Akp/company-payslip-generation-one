About Us

Company Payslip Generation is a web application designed to simplify employee salary management and monthly payslip generation.
The system allows administrators to manage employee details and generate monthly payslips. Users can securely register, log in,
and view their own salary and payslip details.
Main points:
Employee details management
Monthly payslip generation
Admin and user authentication
Users can access their own salary details
Simple and user-friendly interface

Services

Employee Management – Manage employee details.
Payslip Generation – Generate monthly employee payslips.
Salary Management – Manage salary, allowances, and deductions.
User Registration & Login – Allow users to register and log in.
Admin Management – Admin can manage employees and payslips.
Personal Salary Access – Users can view only their own salary details.
